The Artist Behind the Brand

Meet Audrey

Nails by Audrey was born from a genuine love for thoughtful nail care and delicate artistry. Every appointment is designed to feel calm, unhurried, and completely tailored to you.

With a keen eye for detail—from neat cuticle prep and balanced shaping to smooth, long-lasting gel finishes—Audrey believes beautiful nails start with healthy natural nail care and uncompromised precision.

Whether you love sheer minimalist nudes, timeless French tips, or soft feminine pink nail art, your visit is a personalised experience dedicated to leaving you feeling confident and pampered.
